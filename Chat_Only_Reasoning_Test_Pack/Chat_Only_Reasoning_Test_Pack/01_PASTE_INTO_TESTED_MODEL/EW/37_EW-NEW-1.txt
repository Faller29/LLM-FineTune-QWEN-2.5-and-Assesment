(1) In recent years, City A has experienced severe air pollution. The mayor of City A speculates that this situation might be due to the city's booming chemical industry in recent years, which has produced large amounts of industrial emissions. Therefore, the mayor asked the environmental protection department to investigate the gas emissions from various chemical plants last year. The investigation revealed that Shengda Chemical Plant had the highest gas emissions. Thus, the mayor believes that City A's air pollution is mainly caused by emissions from Shengda Chemical Plant.
(2) However, Shengda Chemical Plant denies that their factory emissions are causing City A's air pollution. The factory's employee representative emphasized that Shengda Chemical Plant was among the first batch of factories built in City A, representing the blood and hopes of City A's pioneers. Just as the Daqing Oilfield developed the Daqing Spirit during its construction and development, Shengda Chemical Plant has become a symbol of City A, embodying the spiritual outlook and values of innovation, pioneering, and hard work of City A's people. Therefore, Shengda Chemical Plant should not be held responsible for City A's air pollution.
(3) Subsequently, the mayor conducted a systematic investigation of various industries and possible pollution sources, identifying the chemical industry as the key target for air pollution improvement in the city. Therefore, the mayor required the environmental protection department to establish gas emission standards for chemical plants based on City A's environmental conditions, requiring all chemical plants in the city to reduce production and control their emissions within the standards set by the environmental protection department. The mayor believes this measure will effectively improve City A's air quality.
