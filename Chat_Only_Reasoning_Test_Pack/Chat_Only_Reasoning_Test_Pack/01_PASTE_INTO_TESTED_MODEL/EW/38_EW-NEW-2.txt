Below are some real-life situations where different people present their views and solutions to certain problems. Please consider whether the argumentation in each "numbered paragraph" is strong or weak, and whether there are problems with the views and solutions (such as comprehension bias, logical gaps, inadequate solutions, etc.). Some paragraphs may contain multiple issues. Please analyze and evaluate each paragraph and provide your judgment. All your opinions and judgments need to be supported by reasons.
Each situation contains three paragraphs, so your answer should also have three paragraphs, corresponding to the three paragraphs in the situation.

(1) Xiao Wu runs a clothing store in a commercial district. Recently, sales have been unsatisfactory. During store inspections, Xiao Wu noticed that when there are many customers, staff cannot provide timely service. He speculated that delayed service might be causing customers to lose their desire to purchase. To prove his hypothesis, he randomly interviewed 6 customers who entered the store (4 female customers and 2 male customers). Two female customers and one male customer said that delayed service would indeed "dampen" their desire to purchase, while the remaining customers indicated that service timing did not affect their shopping. Therefore, Xiao Wu concluded that female customers care more about timely service than male customers, so he instructed staff to prioritize serving female customers.

(2) Additionally, Xiao Wu renovated the store because he felt that selecting clothes in a pleasing environment would increase customers' desire to purchase. To verify the effect of this decision, Xiao Wu tracked sales for one week after the renovation, which happened to be during the National Day holiday. Sales increased by 25% compared to the week before renovation, and Xiao Wu was very satisfied with this result, believing it proved his renovation decision was completely correct.

(3) Finally, Xiao Wu recalled that last summer, the store adopted an off-season sales strategy, selling stockpiled leather jackets, wool coats, and down jackets at a discount between June and August, achieving a profit of 100,000 yuan. Based on this, Xiao Wu decided to implement the off-season sales strategy again this winter, selling stockpiled summer clothing at a discount between November and January. He believes this will create another profit peak, achieving or even exceeding 100,000 yuan in profit.
