(1) There are increasingly more people riding electric bicycles, leading to crowded non-motorized vehicle lanes. Electric bicycles often cross the boundaries of non-motorized lanes into motorized vehicle lanes, which is very dangerous. The busy city center already experiences frequent traffic accidents - in the past year alone, there were over 100 traffic accidents in the city center. If electric bicycles could be banned from these streets, the number of accidents in the city center would greatly decrease.
(2) An internet user compiled statistics on the number of traffic accidents in our city center and the purchase volume of electric bicycles by our city residents over the past three years, finding that both have been increasing year by year. This shows that the increase in electric bicycles has indeed led to worse traffic conditions, making residents' travel less safe, so it is necessary to restrict electric bicycles from entering the city center.
(3) Electric bicycles themselves pose safety risks. We've all heard news about electric bicycles causing fires while charging in residential buildings. One could say that an electric bicycle battery while charging is no different from a bomb that could explode at any time. Having these "bombs" in the crowded, bustling city center could have unimaginable consequences if danger occurs. Therefore, I strongly recommend banning electric bicycles on busy streets in the city center.
