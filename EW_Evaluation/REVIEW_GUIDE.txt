EVIDENCE-BASED EW REVIEW

Use review_packet.txt, the exact raw responses, and the source manual.
The template binds the review to run_config.json and responses.jsonl hashes.
Do not change those hashes to apply an older review to newer responses.
If generation is resumed, use its freshly rebuilt template.

1. Identify reviewer_name and reviewer_type (human or Manual).
2. Retain every case and every unit. A weak/missing answer still needs a rating.
3. Change each completed rating's status from pending to reviewed.
4. Assign integer base_points: 0..3, except original P9 which is 0..5.
   Base credit measures alignment with the released reference criterion.
   Give partial credit according to completeness and adequacy of the support.
   Equivalent wording can receive credit; an unrelated but nonerroneous
   response receives no reference-alignment credit under the released manual.
5. Cite exact evidence from the raw model response and explain the rating.
   Positive credit requires a nonempty exact excerpt. Zero credit can use an
   empty excerpt when the model omitted the required content, but still needs
   a written reason. The script checks provenance, not the correctness of
   the grader's semantic judgment.
6. Complete original P9's four closing_components with credited true/false,
   evidence, and a reason. Its base points must equal the weighted sum:
     overall_judgment: 1
     paragraph_summary: 1
     scope_overgeneralization: 2
     emotive_language: 1
   The source permits the last two insights anywhere in the original response.
   The first two concern the overall closing evaluation. Specify location in
   your reason; the validator permits excerpts anywhere in the original raw
   response because paragraph numbering can be imperfect.
7. Review paragraph_errors once per response paragraph, including the closing
   paragraph. Do not duplicate a single error across both flaws in one paragraph.
   Set status reviewed and enter a comment even when there are no errors.
   Each error has:
     kind: invented_flaw / endorsed_flawed_argument / faulty_reasoning /
           unsupported_claim / scope_omission / other_manual_error
     evidence: exact excerpt from the model response
     explanation: why the error warrants a deduction
     is_omission: false (true permits empty evidence for genuinely absent content)
   Count separate errors only when they are genuinely separate instances.
8. For original P3 and P8, scope_qualification_present must be true or false.
   The released manual explicitly requires a one-point deduction when their
   scope qualification is missing. The scorer adds this deduction unless a
   scope_omission error has already been recorded for that paragraph. Explain
   this judgment in the paragraph's comment. Do not double-count the omission.
9. Review the complete argument, not just its first numbered paragraph.
   Missing sections are evaluated as omissions, not silently dropped from the
   denominator. Do not penalize spelling, style, absent thinking tags, or lack
   of specialist vocabulary unless these obscure the substantive judgment.
10. Import the completed JSON with apply-review. Never invent a critical-thinking
    pass cutoff or label a score as proof of a faithful internal thought process.

SCORING FORMULAS
Let b_i be reference-alignment credit for each of 21 units.
Let e_p be the documented error count for each of 18 response paragraphs,
including the released manual's explicit scope-omission deductions.

Base alignment B = sum_i b_i. Maximum = 65.
Released-manual deductions D_manual = sum_p min(1, e_p).
Primary project score S_manual = B - D_manual.
Article-wording sensitivity score S_article = B - sum_p e_p.

The two scores are separate views of the same documented review, not two tests.
They address a discrepancy between the released manual and article wording.
No undocumented zero-floor is applied to deductions; negative paragraph/total
scores remain visible. Error count and alignment credit are also reported
separately. Missing qualifications can incur deductions even with zero credit;
this explicit operationalization follows the manual's omission instruction.

MULTIPLE RATERS / ROUNDS
Each completed reviewer has a total for each full round. The script reports
their individual totals and a descriptive mean across supplied reviewers.
Across actually completed sampled rounds, it can report mean and sample SD.
A single reviewer supplies no inter-rater reliability; no ICC is invented.
An AI-assisted review should be checked by a knowledgeable human. A second
reviewer should score independently before seeing the first review.
