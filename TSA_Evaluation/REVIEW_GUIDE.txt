REVIEWING QWEN TSA RESPONSES

Purpose: inspect answer extraction and the validity of written explanations.
Primary test: the 35 original TSA questions, scored against published UCLES keys.
Supplemental review: documented AI-assisted or human judgments; no extra TSA points.

For the ChatGPT reviewer

Read the entire question, every option, the reference key, and the RAW response.
Do not rate a response based only on the extracted letter or fluent writing.
The sources give keys; a published explanation is not supplied for every item.
Use the question's actual argument to justify your review. If a key appears
disputable, flag it for independent checking; do not silently rewrite the key.

Keep correct-answer performance separate from explanation validity. A correct
choice can have a flawed rationale. A response can contain a sound observation
without identifying the correct option. Thinking tags alone receive no credit.

Answer audit

answer_status:
  clear:     The response unambiguously states one selected option.
  ambiguous: It leaves conflicting options without a clear resolution.
  missing:   It gives no selected option.
  pending:   This item has not yet been reviewed.

For clear answers, record choice_as_written as A/B/C/D/E and answer_evidence as
an exact excerpt of the MODEL RESPONSE that establishes that selection.
For ambiguous, missing or pending answers, choice_as_written must be null.
Do not choose the gold letter on behalf of the model. Do not count an option
quoted merely as a rejected alternative as the model's final selection.
If a choice is only inside an unfinished thinking section, document that fact
and the uncertainty rather than automatically treating it as a final answer.

Explanation audit

explanation_status:
  supported:    The stated justification identifies the relevant relationship
                and supports the selected answer without a material error.
  flawed:       It contains a material invalid inference, contradiction,
                invented premise, or misreading.
  insufficient: It states an answer or repeats words without enough reasoning
                to assess the justification.
  missing:      There is no explanatory content.
  unclear:      The explanation cannot be judged confidently; explain why.
  pending:      Not yet reviewed.

These categories describe the written explanation. They do not establish
whether it faithfully reports the model's internal process.
An incorrect selected answer will generally not have a supported justification
for that choice. Credit an accurate partial observation in the comment while
retaining the material flaw or insufficiency category.

Criterion-specific checks

Main conclusion: distinguishes the main claim from a supporting reason,
background detail, example or subordinate conclusion.
Drawing a conclusion: supports the conclusion with what the passage supplies;
does not overgeneralize or import unsupported information.
Assumption: identifies a required unstated link, rather than an optional
additional fact or a restatement of the conclusion.
Additional evidence: explains the relevant effect on the argument, rather
than treating any interesting information as support.
Reasoning errors: identifies the actual error and its consequence for the
conclusion; does not invent a different flaw.
Matching arguments: identifies logical structure, including invalid structure
when present, rather than merely matching topics or wording.
Applying principles: applies the same principle and its relevant conditions
consistently, rather than choosing the most familiar or agreeable option.

Evidence and review record

Use review_template.json as the schema.
Fill reviewer_name honestly (for example, "ChatGPT, AI-assisted review"),
and reviewer_type as Manual or human.
Preserve run_config_sha256, responses_sha256 and all response IDs.
For supported/flawed/insufficient/unclear explanations, supply an exact excerpt
in explanation_evidence and a substantive comment explaining the judgment.
If no explanation exists, use missing and state that in the comment.
Keep unreviewed entries pending rather than assigning them zero or flawed.

Review each response on its own merits. Do not favor the target model, verbosity,
polished language, a particular answer letter, or a high overall score.
Check disagreements against the source paper/key and supplied passage.
Flag genuinely ambiguous judgments for a human reviewer.

Return a readable account of each question's points (0/1), explanation judgment,
and justification, plus a filled JSON ratings file if asked.
If response extraction was wrong, preserve the original automatic score and
show the separately audited score and exact corrections.
Do not add the explanation audit to the TSA points or create a "thinking score."

Presentation

Report the answer-key total and seven criterion totals, extraction failures,
budget/truncation flags, and explanation-status counts. Use exact examples for
claims about the kinds of errors or successful reasoning demonstrated.
State that the review was AI-assisted. For stronger formal evidence, obtain an
independent human review of ambiguous cases and a predefined sample or all items.
