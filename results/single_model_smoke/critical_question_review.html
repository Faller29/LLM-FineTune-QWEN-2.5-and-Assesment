Q10

Read the argument below. Generate exactly THREE specific questions that could expose unsupported assumptions, weak evidence, or relevant alternative explanations. Do not answer the questions.
Argument: No one in our small team has disproved claim K, so claim K has been proved true.

Model response

1. **Question 1:** Is it possible for a single individual to disprove a widely accepted scientific theory?
2. **Question 2:** How can we ensure that all members of a team are equally qualified and capable of making independent claims about a topic?
3. **Question 3:** What is the validity of the claim "No one in our small team has disproved claim K" if we consider the possibility of multiple individuals within the team potentially being able to refute the claim?
Reviewer guidance

Absence of disproof is not sufficient positive proof.

  • What positive evidence supports K?
  • Was K tested adequately?
  • Could the team have lacked the evidence or resources needed to test K?

These are examples, not mandatory wording. Other valid, specific questions can be useful.
